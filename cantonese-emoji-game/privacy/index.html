私隱政策

Privacy Policy · 生效日期 Effective date: 2026年10月3日

我們收集的資料 · Data We Collect

本 App 不要求建立帳戶，亦不會由開發者收集、儲存或傳送你的個人資料。本 App 不使用分析、追蹤或第三方廣告 SDK。

This app does not require an account and does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to the developer. It does not use analytics, tracking, or third-party advertising SDKs.

裝置上的資料 · Data Stored on Your Device

遊戲進度、遊戲統計、語言、外觀設定及購買狀態會儲存在你的裝置上，用於提供 App 功能。你可以在 App 內重設遊戲進度，亦可以刪除 App 以移除其本機資料。

Game progress, gameplay statistics, language, appearance preferences, and purchase status are stored locally on your device to provide app functionality. You can reset game progress inside the app or delete the app to remove its local data.

App 內購買 · In-App Purchases

移除廣告購買由 Apple 透過 StoreKit 處理。開發者不會收到或儲存你的付款卡資料。Apple 對購買資料的處理受 Apple 私隱政策約束。

The remove-ads purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive or store your payment-card details. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

外部連結 · External Links

App 可能會連結至 Apple App Store 上的其他 App。離開本 App 後，相關服務的私隱政策將適用。

The app may link to other apps on the Apple App Store. Once you leave this app, the privacy policy of the relevant service applies.

兒童私隱 · Children’s Privacy

由於本 App 不會由開發者收集個人資料，因此亦不會在知情情況下收集兒童的個人資料。

Because the app does not collect personal data for the developer, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

政策更新 · Changes to This Policy

如果 App 的資料處理方式改變，本政策將會更新，並修改生效日期。

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date will be revised.

聯絡 · Contact

如對本私隱政策有任何疑問，請透過 GitHub Issues 聯絡開發者。

For privacy questions, contact the developer through GitHub Issues.
