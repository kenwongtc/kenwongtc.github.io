私隱政策

Privacy Policy · 生效日期 Effective date: 2026年10月3日

我們收集的資料 · Data We Collect

本 App 不要求建立帳戶，開發者亦不會直接要求你提供姓名、電郵地址或付款卡資料。不過，本 App 使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告；Google 及其廣告合作夥伴可能會按你的同意選擇及所在地區，處理裝置識別碼、IP 位址、廣告互動、診斷資料及大概位置等資料，以提供、衡量及改善廣告服務。

This app does not require an account, and the developer does not directly ask you for your name, email address, or payment-card details. However, the app uses Google AdMob to display ads. Depending on your consent choices and region, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

廣告及同意選擇 · Advertising and Consent Choices

首次使用 App 時或法律要求的情況下，你可能會看到由 Google 提供的同意訊息，讓你選擇同意、拒絕或管理廣告私隱選項。可用時，你亦可以在 App 的「設定」內開啟「私隱選項」，更改或撤回選擇。拒絕個人化廣告不代表完全不會顯示廣告；你仍可能看到非個人化或有限度廣告。

When you first use the app, or where legally required, you may see a Google-provided consent message allowing you to consent, decline, or manage advertising privacy choices. Where available, you can also open “Privacy Choices” in the app’s Settings to change or withdraw your choices. Declining personalised advertising does not necessarily remove all ads; you may still receive non-personalised or limited ads.

詳情請參閱 Google 私隱政策及 Google 使用合作夥伴資料的說明。

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

裝置上的資料 · Data Stored on Your Device

遊戲進度、遊戲統計、語言、外觀設定及購買狀態會儲存在你的裝置上，用於提供 App 功能。你可以在 App 內重設遊戲進度，亦可以刪除 App 以移除其本機資料。

Game progress, gameplay statistics, language, appearance preferences, and purchase status are stored locally on your device to provide app functionality. You can reset game progress inside the app or delete the app to remove its local data.

App 內購買 · In-App Purchases

可選擇的一次性「移除廣告」購買由 Apple 透過 StoreKit 處理。開發者不會收到或儲存你的付款卡資料。App 會讀取購買資格，以移除廣告及支援「恢復購買」。Apple 對購買資料的處理受 Apple 私隱政策約束。

The optional one-time “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive or store your payment-card details. The app reads purchase entitlement information to remove ads and support Restore Purchases. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

資料保留及控制 · Data Retention and Control

儲存在裝置上的資料會保留至你在 App 內重設相關資料或刪除 App 為止。由 Google 或 Apple 處理的資料，其保留、存取及刪除安排受各服務供應商的政策及工具約束。

Data stored on your device remains until you reset the relevant data in the app or delete the app. Information processed by Google or Apple is subject to each provider’s retention, access, and deletion policies and controls.

外部連結 · External Links

App 可能會連結至 Apple App Store 上的其他 App。離開本 App 後，相關服務的私隱政策將適用。

The app may link to other apps on the Apple App Store. Once you leave this app, the privacy policy of the relevant service applies.

兒童私隱 · Children’s Privacy

本 App 並非專為兒童而設。開發者不會在知情情況下直接收集兒童提供的個人資料。廣告服務會按適用法律、使用者同意選擇及 Google 的政策運作。

The app is not specifically directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information submitted directly by children. Advertising services operate according to applicable law, the user’s consent choices, and Google’s policies.

政策更新 · Changes to This Policy

如果 App 的資料處理方式改變，本政策將會更新，並修改生效日期。

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date will be revised.

聯絡 · Contact

如對本私隱政策有任何疑問，請透過 GitHub Issues 聯絡開發者。

For privacy questions, contact the developer through GitHub Issues.
